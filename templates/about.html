The Studio

About {{ product_name }}

{{ product_name }} is a high-precision, privacy-first translation studio for seamlessly moving between English, Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Spanish, and French. It is built to feel premium, respond instantly, and connect with authentic translation providers — never producing fake or hardcoded text.

Indian-language translations are handled by Sarvam AI, and European pairs are powered by MyMemory. All your history, settings, and personal preferences remain private on your computer. There is no cloud tracking, no sign-up wall, and no data profiling.

Key Capabilities

  • Two-Panel Translation Studio: Ergonomic source and destination panels with real-time word counting and language swapping.
  • Voice Dictation & Speech Playback: Dictate directly via browser speech recognition and listen to accurate native pronunciations.
  • Smart Language Detection: Automatic linguistic detection so you don't have to manually pick the source tongue.
  • Private On-Device History: Full SQLite translation journal stored locally, with quick reuse and one-click deletion.
  • Aurora Studio Aesthetics: Tailored dark and light themes, 3D rotating multilingual Earth visualization, and fluid glassmorphism.
