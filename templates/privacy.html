Data & Security

Privacy Policy

{{ product_name }} is designed with a local-first philosophy. We believe your translations and notes belong exclusively to you.

1. Zero Account Requirement

You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address, password, or phone number to use this translator.

2. Local SQLite Storage

All your translation history, timestamps, and language settings are stored directly in a local SQLite database file on your own computer. This data is never synchronized to a central company cloud or sold to third parties.

3. Local Browser Preferences

Your name and display theme preference (Dark Aurora or Light Dawn) are preserved in your browser’s local storage and session cookies so you don’t have to re-enter them on every visit. You can update or delete your saved name at any time in Settings.

4. Voice & Speech Recognition

Microphone dictation and speech playback use your browser's built-in Web Speech API. Audio data is not recorded or retained on our server.

5. Translation Requests

When you submit text for translation, the engine transmits only the text snippet necessary to generate the translation. No tracking cookies or personal profile identifiers are attached to translation requests.
